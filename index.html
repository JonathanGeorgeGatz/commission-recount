The Commission Recount

Paste one month of deals, your comp plan tiers, and what you actually paid. It recomputes every rep's commission with marginal tiers — splits, pending set aside, refunds clawed back at the rep's share — and shows who was overpaid and who was shorted. Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. sample data is fictional

Rules, prompts, the Python referee and the two failed drafts are in the download folder next to this page. The plan this tool implements is written in SPEC.md.
