THE THREE PROMPTS — exactly as sent during the build (Episode 29, The Commission Recount)

=== PROMPT 1 ===
I pay my garage door sales reps tiered commissions and I think my spreadsheet is paying some of
them wrong. Attached are two CSVs: one month of deals (deal id, close date, rep, amount, status,
and a Share % column for split deals) and what I actually paid each rep. Build me a single-file
web page I can open in my browser that reads both files, works out each rep's commission for the
month, compares it to what I paid, and shows me who got the wrong amount. No servers, no uploads,
no accounts — everything runs in the browser.

=== PROMPT 2 ===
Rebuild it, and this time implement my comp plan exactly as written. The plan:

- A rep's credit for the month is the sum of deal amount times their Share % over the deals
  COLLECTED that month. Shares on a deal must total exactly 100; if they don't, refuse every row
  of that deal and say why.
- Deals with status "pending" were sold but not collected. Set them aside and list them — they
  are worth nothing yet.
- A deal with status "refunded" claws back at the same share: it SUBTRACTS amount times Share %
  from that rep's credit.
- Commission uses MARGINAL tiers on the month's credit: 4% on the dollars up to and including
  $40,000, 6% on the dollars above $40,000 up to and including $80,000, and 8% on the dollars
  above $80,000. Each rate applies only to the dollars inside its band — reaching a tier never
  changes the rate on the dollars below it. A credit of exactly $40,000 sits entirely in the
  first band.
- Negative credit means $0 commission this month.
- Find every column by its header name, in any order. Refuse any row you cannot read — an amount
  with letters in it, a status that isn't collected/pending/refunded, a missing rep or deal id,
  a duplicate row — with the row and a reason. Never guess.
- Compare each rep's correct commission to the Paid table and show the difference.

=== PROMPT 3 ===
Before anyone reads the report, make the page audit itself. Rows in must equal checked plus set
aside plus refused. Add the credited money up two independent ways and compare to the cent. If
any check fails, or more than ten percent of the rows were refused, or the tier settings make no
sense, open the report with DO NOT TRUST THIS RECOUNT in red and price nothing. Keep the page a
single file with zero network requests and zero stored data.
